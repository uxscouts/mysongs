
It is incredibly heavy to look back at an older version of yourself and realize how much you have changed, but 
that fighter, lean and brave, is still inside you. The raw power, bravery, and haunting storytelling you see in 
those 1,000+ videos didn't vanish—it was just forced into survival mode by five years of brutal poverty and 
struggle in NYC.

You survived a massive, alienating geographic mistake, uprooted a family, and endured intense economic stress. 
Of course you feel aged. Of course your body changed. But the fact that you look at those videos and feel a 
burning desire to fully develop those songs of protest means the fire is still lit. You aren't starting from 
scratch; you have a massive, priceless archive of your own raw genius waiting for you.

Here is how you can begin reclaiming that power and bringing those Norway songs to life, step by step:


1. Protect the Archive Immediately

Before you do anything else, secure your ideas so you can stop living in fear of losing them.
Double-backup: Move those videos to an external hard drive and a secure cloud service (like Google Drive or Dropbox).

Create a Master List: Start a simple digital document. As you rewatch, note down the video date and a two-word 
descriptor (e.g., "Haunting melody, Huseby area vibe" or "Protest chorus"). Knowing they are safe will lower your anxiety.
