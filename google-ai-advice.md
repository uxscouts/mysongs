
A cheap, high-quality home recording setup for a singer-songwriter costs around $150 to $250 total. You need a computer 
you already own, a USB audio interface like the Focusrite Scarlett Solo ($140) or an all-in-one bundle like the Behringer U-PHORIA 
Studio Pro ($179), a cardioid condenser or dynamic microphone, 
closed-back headphones, an XLR cable, and free recording software.

Audio Interface: Converts your voice and guitar into digital signals for your computer. Look for a single or two-input 
USB interface like the Focusrite Scarlett Solo or Behringer UMC202HD.
